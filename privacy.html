Privacy policy

Effective October 18, 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Tekla Studio has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no server-side storage. The formulas, templates, reports and mappings you type are never transmitted anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your saved formulas, custom tag mappings, active editor session and theme choice are stored in your browser's IndexedDB database named TeklaStudioDB. A service worker also keeps a copy of the application files so the app runs offline. You can remove all of it by clearing site data in your browser settings.

Backups and sharing

The Export workspace button creates a .json file on your device. Whether you share that file, and with whom, is your decision. Importing a file only reads it locally in your browser.

Third parties

The app loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers. Your hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs of requests for the app files, under that provider's own policy.

Confidential project data

Because processing is local, you can use real project attributes and report output. Review exported backups before sharing them outside your company.

Changes and questions

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date. For questions, open an issue in the project repository.
